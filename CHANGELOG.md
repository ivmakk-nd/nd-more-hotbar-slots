## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-30

### Added

- The Small Backpack adds 4 hotbar slots instead of 1, and the Large Backpack adds 8 instead of 2, for up to 10 hotbar slots.
- Each backpack stores as many items as the hotbar slots it adds.
- Keys 9 and 0 select hotbar slots 9 and 10.
- Settings for the base slots, the slots of each backpack, and the two keys.
